What happens after you send this

You will get a reply within two working days. If there is a fit, the next step is a 30-minute call to understand the business, the funnel and what has already been tried. After that we send a short written scope with the work, the timeline and the fee. No long pitch decks.

What to include in your message

The more context you give, the more useful the first call is. Helpful details:

  • Your website and product category.
  • Monthly revenue range and current ad spend, if you are comfortable sharing it.
  • The channel that brings most of your orders today.
  • The specific problem: flat growth, rising CPA, weak repeat rate, poor search visibility.
  • What a good outcome would look like in six months.

Who you will be talking to

Enquiries are read by the strategist who would run the work, not a sales team. If we are not the right fit, we will tell you on the first call and, where we can, point you to someone better suited.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will you reply?

Within two working days. If you have not heard back in three, assume something went wrong and send the message again.

Is there a minimum engagement?

Paid media and search retainers usually run for a minimum of three months, because that is the shortest period in which a test cycle can produce a readable result.

Do you work with brands outside India?

Selectively. Our focus is Indian D2C, because platform behaviour, pricing and creative norms are specific to the market.
