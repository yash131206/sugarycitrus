Pricing

What this costs.

Published, because a price on a page is a fact and "on request" is a dead end. These are monthly fees for our work. Ad spend and production sit outside them and are paid directly by you.

The three retainers

Paid mediaMeta Ads or Google Ads, one channel
Rs 8,000 – 15,000per month
Creative strategyBriefs, scripts, testing system
Rs 5,000 – 10,000per month
SEO, AEO and GEOSearch and AI visibility
Rs 7,000 – 15,000per month

Take all three and the fee combines; it is not a discount, it is three pieces of work. Most brands start with one channel and add the rest once the basics hold.

What the fee includes

  • Diagnosis, plan and the written priority list at the start.
  • Day-to-day management, testing and iteration.
  • A written monthly readout: what changed, what it cost, what happens next.
  • Direct access to me — no account manager in between.

What it does not include

  • Ad spend. Paid directly to Meta or Google from your own account. You keep ownership.
  • Production. Shoot, creator fees, editing and asset costs are paid to creators or production partners directly.
  • Tools. Any paid software the work needs, agreed before it is bought.

What moves the number

  • Ad spend. A Rs 1 lakh a month account needs more management than a Rs 25,000 one.
  • Number of channels. One channel is one retainer. Meta plus Google is two pieces of work.
  • Catalogue size and markets. More SKUs and more regions mean more feed and structure work.
  • How much writing we do. Search work is priced by content volume.

Minimum engagement

Three months for paid media and search. That is the shortest period in which a test cycle produces a readable result, and a shorter engagement would be us taking money for noise.

What is not negotiable

We do not guarantee ROAS, rankings, traffic volumes or AI citations, and we do not discount the fee to win work. If the number does not work for your budget, the honest answer is that we are probably not the right fit yet — and we will say so on the call.

Frequently asked questions

Why publish prices at all?

Because a range saves everyone a call. Most agency sites hide the number, which means the first conversation is a budget check rather than a problem. A published range filters better than any qualification form.

Is the fee a percentage of ad spend?

No. It is a flat monthly retainer inside the range above, agreed after scoping. Percentage-of-spend pricing rewards spending more, which is the wrong incentive for a small account.

Can we start with a one-off project?

Yes. Audits, creative strategy sprints and technical fixes can be scoped as one-off projects instead of retainers. Ask on the call.

What if our budget is lower than this?

Then a full retainer is the wrong shape right now. A one-off audit is the better first buy, and it usually pays for itself before a retainer makes sense.
