About Sugary Citrus

Sugary Citrus is a performance marketing agency for Indian D2C brands in fashion, beauty, skincare, personal care and lifestyle. Today it is one person, and that is a choice rather than a limitation. It will change when the work can support a team, not before.

In most agencies, the person who wins the pitch is not the person who runs your account. Here they are the same person. There is no account manager between you and the work.

How it works

Every engagement starts with a diagnosis, not a deck. We look at the numbers you already have, the offer, the funnel and the creative, and write down what we think is actually holding growth back. Only then do we propose work.

  • Diagnose: account, analytics and funnel review, plus a short conversation about the business model.
  • Plan: a written priority list with the expected effort and the metric each change should move.
  • Build: campaigns, pages, briefs and tracking, shipped in small batches.
  • Review: a monthly written readout of what changed, what it cost and what happens next.

Where we are useful, and where we are not

We are most useful to Indian D2C brands between roughly Rs 1 crore and Rs 50 crore in annual revenue, with a product that already sells and a team that can ship changes.

We are not a full-service agency. We do not do PR, offline media, packaging design or marketplace catalogue operations at scale. If your problem sits outside paid media, creative systems and search visibility, we will say so rather than stretch.

What we will not do

We do not guarantee ROAS, rankings, traffic or AI citations. We do not publish client names, numbers or testimonials without written permission and a verifiable method. And we do not pad reports with activity metrics that do not connect to money.

Frequently asked questions

Who runs the work?

Yash Sharma, the founder, leads every engagement and stays on the account. The client list is kept short so that stays true.

Do you work on retainer or project basis?

Both. Paid media and search work usually run as monthly retainers; audits, creative strategy sprints and technical fixes are scoped as one-off projects.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Yes. I usually take media buying, measurement and strategy while you keep production, catalogue or development in-house.

What do you need to start?

Read-only access to your ad accounts and analytics, one call about the business model, and a month of clean data. Diagnosis usually takes about a week.
