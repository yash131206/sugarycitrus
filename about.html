A little about us

Good marketing.
Good thinking.

Sugary Citrus is an independent digital growth agency built around a simple belief: effective marketing starts with understanding the business, not chasing the next trend.

Our philosophy

Human insight.
Commercial intent.

We help ambitious brands make clearer marketing decisions. That means understanding customer behaviour, finding a compelling message, building a strong creative system and measuring what actually moves the business.

We value honest communication, focused experiments and work that can stand up to scrutiny. We do not manufacture results or dress up activity as impact.

What we stand for

Curiosity over assumptions.
Evidence over ego.

Our job is to make the complex feel clear and the next step feel actionable.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a diagnosis, not a deck. We look at the numbers you already have, the offer, the funnel and the creative, and write down what we think is actually holding growth back. Only then do we propose work.

  • Diagnose: account, analytics and funnel review, plus a short conversation about the business model.
  • Plan: a written priority list with expected effort and the metric each change should move.
  • Build: campaigns, pages, briefs and tracking, shipped in small batches.
  • Review: a monthly written readout of what changed, what it cost and what happens next.

Where we are useful, and where we are not

We are most useful to Indian D2C brands between roughly Rs 1 crore and Rs 50 crore in annual revenue, with a product that already sells and a team that can ship changes. Categories we know well include fashion, beauty, skincare, personal care and lifestyle.

We are not a full-service agency. We do not do PR, offline media, packaging design or marketplace catalogue operations at scale. If your problem sits outside paid media, creative systems and search visibility, we will say so rather than stretch.

What we will not do

We do not guarantee ROAS, rankings, traffic or AI citations. We do not publish client names, numbers or testimonials without written permission and a verifiable method. And we do not pad reports with activity metrics that do not connect to money.

Frequently asked questions

Who runs the work?

A senior strategist leads every engagement and stays on the account. We keep the number of active clients small so that stays true.

Do you work on retainer or project basis?

Both. Paid media and search work usually run as monthly retainers; audits, creative strategy sprints and technical fixes can be scoped as one-off projects.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Yes. Several clients keep production, marketplace operations or development in-house and use us for strategy, media buying and measurement.
